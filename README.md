# My Favorite Movies and Series

## 🕷️ Spider-Man (Movies)

*Spider-Man* is a superhero created by **Stan Lee** and **Steve Ditko**. He first appeared in 1962.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

**Why I like it:**

- Peter Parker is an *relatable* hero
- Great action and web-swinging scenes
- Funny and emotional at the same time

## 🦖 Godzilla (Movies)

The first *Godzilla* film was released in **1954** by Toho in Japan. It is one of the longest-running movie franchises ever.

1. Giant monsters
2. Epic city-destroying fights
3. A classic that is still made today

## 🧟 The Walking Dead (Series)

*The Walking Dead* is an AMC series based on a comic book. It ran for **11 seasons** (2010–2022).

- [x] Watched the series
- [x] Love the survival story
- [ ] Finish watching the spin-offs

## ⚡ The Flash (Series)

*The Flash* follows **Barry Allen**, a scientist who gets super speed. Barry is played by Grant Gustin.
